# UK Single Garage Dimensions: Old vs New Sizes

There is **no single national minimum size** for a garage in the UK. In practice, garages fall into two broad groups.

| Type | Typical internal size | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Older single garage | about 2.4m wide × 4.9m long | [Homebuilding & Renovating](https://www.homebuilding.co.uk/advice/garage-size) |
| Newer single garage (counts as a parking space) | 3.0m × 6.0m | Council parking standards, e.g. [North Lanarkshire](https://www.northlanarkshire.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2023-12/Residential%20Parking%20Provision.pdf) |
| Some councils | 3.0m × 7.0m | e.g. [Essex Design Guide](https://www.essexdesignguide.co.uk/design-details/2024-essex-parking-guidance/residents-parking/) |

## Why it matters for storage

Many popular cars now measure over 2m wide with mirrors. In an older 2.4m garage that leaves almost no room to open a door, let alone fit shelving beside the car. In a 3m × 6m garage, the back wall typically has space for a 600mm deep shelving run.

## Measuring a garage properly

1. **Width:** inside wall to inside wall, at the narrowest point (piers and downpipes count).
2. **Length:** from the inside of the door frame to the back wall.
3. **Usable height:** to the lowest point of an up-and-over door track where shelving will stand.
